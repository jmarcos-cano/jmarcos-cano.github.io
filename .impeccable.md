## Design Context

### Users
Primary: hiring managers and recruiters who skim for seniority and a clear signal, then email or open LinkedIn. Secondary: fellow platform engineers who read the case studies closely and judge technical taste. The page must work at a glance first, and reward a slower read.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, human. Voice is plain and specific: decisions and outcomes, no internal names, no exact spend. The emotional goal is quiet confidence, the sense of someone who draws the system before building it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Blueprint / engineering drawing. Fine drafting grid, annotated diagrams, dimension-style labels, hairline rules. Light theme is primary (paper-like, tinted toward the blueprint hue), with a considered dark mode that follows the OS. Anti-references: generic dev-portfolio templates, glowing dark "hacker" pages, SaaS gradients, pill-chip and card-grid sameness. Avoid monospace as lazy shorthand for "technical": use it only where it carries real drafting meaning (dimensions, callouts).

### Design Principles
1. Draw, don't decorate. Every ornament (grid, tick, callout) must behave like a real drawing convention and carry information.
2. Recruiter-first scan path. Name, thesis, three headline outcomes and a contact action are visible without scrolling; depth sits below.
3. One expressive idea, everything else quiet. The platform diagram is the hero artifact; type and spacing stay restrained.
4. Distinctive type, tinted neutrals. No Inter/Roboto, no pure black or white, no gradient text.
5. Respect the reader: accessible contrast, reduced-motion safe, works at phone width, content stays in `src/data/site.ts`.
